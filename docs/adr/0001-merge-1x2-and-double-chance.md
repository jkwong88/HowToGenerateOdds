# Merge 1X2 and Double Chance into one page

Double Chance is just 1X2's three probabilities summed in pairs (1X, 12, X2) — it has no scoreline logic of its own. Rather than give it a standalone page (like every other market), 3.3 covers both as two gated parts on one page: Part 1 (1X2) unlocks Part 2 (Double Chance), the same gating pattern Historical Match Data already uses for its three parts. This avoids a page whose entire content is "add two numbers from the previous page together."
