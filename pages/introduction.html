How To Open Odds

Seven self-checking exercises that walk through how a sportsbook derives its football odds from raw match data, one step at a time.

What you'll learn

  • How raw match history turns into goal-count frequencies and probabilities
  • How to build a joint Home × Away scoreline probability matrix
  • How a Correct Score board converts probabilities into Euro, HK, and Malay odds
  • How an Odd / Even market is priced from that same scoreline grid
  • How an Over / Under market is classified and priced, including the push and half-win/half-lose cases at quarter points
  • How a bookmaker sweeps every point to find where to actually open an Over / Under market
  • How a spread shifts a fair price into what's actually quoted
