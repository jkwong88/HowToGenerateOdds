Correct Score Probability & Odds

"Any Other Score (AOS)" covers every scoreline outside the 5×5 Home/Away grid above. In this dataset, AOS works out to 0.00 — not because those scorelines are impossible, but because none of the 10 historical matches happened to include one. A historical-frequency model can only assign probability to what it has actually observed, so a small sample can make a real possibility look like zero. Section 5 (Poisson) shows one way to fix that.
