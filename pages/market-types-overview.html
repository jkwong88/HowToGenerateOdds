3. Odds for Different Market Types

After generated final score probability matrix, every market below is just a different way of grouping and summing cells from that same matrix, then converting the result into a price.

Knowledge Needed Before Working Through Them

The Given Data

Every market in this section takes the Home × Away scoreline matrix from section 2 as given data — nothing here recomputes it.

Settlement Logic

Each market groups and sums matrix cells that satisfy its win condition (e.g. Home + Away is odd, Home > Away, Total Goals falls in a bucket). Scorelines that don't settle as a clean win/lose — a push or a half-win/half-lose — are set aside before converting a category's probability into a price (Selection probability).

The Odds Conversion Chain

Fair Probability → Euro → HK → Malay.

Euro Odds

1 / Probability

HK Odds

Euro − 1

Malay Odds

HK if HK ≤ 1, else −1 / HK
