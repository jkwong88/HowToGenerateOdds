3. Odds for Different Market Types

Once the final score probability matrix is built, each betting market is simply a different way of grouping and summing scoreline probabilities. Those probabilities can then be converted into odds.

What You Need to Know First

Starting Point

Every market in this section starts from the Final Score Probability Matrix built in Section 2. The matrix itself is not recalculated.

Settlement Logic

Each market groups scorelines according to its settlement rules, then sums the corresponding probabilities. For lines that can result in a push or split settlement, the middle outcome is adjusted according to its settlement value before the final bettable probability is calculated.

The Odds Conversion Chain

Fair Probability → Euro → HK → Malay.

Euro Odds

1 / Probability

HK Odds

Euro − 1

Malay Odds

HK if HK ≤ 1, else −1 / HK
