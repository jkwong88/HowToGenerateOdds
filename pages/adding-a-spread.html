4.2 Adding a Pricing Spread

Why We Spread the Malay Odds

A quoted market isn't fair — the bookmaker builds in a margin. This course applies that margin by shaving a pricing spread directly off the fair Malay odds, half on each side, rather than adjusting probability directly.

Because the spread is applied to price rather than probability, converting the shaved Malay odds back to a probability no longer gives a fair split — the two sides' implied probabilities now sum to slightly more than 100%. That excess is the overround: it's how the bookmaker's margin shows up in the numbers, regardless of the match outcome.
