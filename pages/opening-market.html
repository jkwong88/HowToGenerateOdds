4. Opening Market

A fair market can be built for any point or line, but a bookmaker only ever lists a few. This section covers how a specific line gets chosen (opened) from everything section 3 makes possible, and how it's priced once it's actually quoted.

4.1 Opening the Market

Sweep every point (Over/Under, 0.5–4) or line (Handicap, -2 to 2) using the same math as 3.5/3.6, and find whichever one(s) split the two BetTeam probabilities closest to 50/50. That's the fairest, most balanced line to open — it draws the most two-way action, since neither side looks like the obviously better bet.

4.2 Adding a Spread

Take the line 4.1 opened and shave a spread symmetrically off its fair Malay odds (half off each side). This is how a real, quoted market differs from a fair one: the resulting probabilities now sum to slightly more than 100%, and that excess is the bookmaker's margin.
